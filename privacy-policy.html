Privacy Policy for FreePDFHub

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

At FreePDFHub (freepdfhub.vercel.app), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. No Data Collection

FreePDFHub is a 100% client-side tool. All your JPG to PDF conversions happen directly in your browser using JavaScript. We do NOT upload your images or PDFs to any server. Your files never leave your device.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand visitor traffic. This is privacy-friendly and does not use cookies to track you across sites. We may also use Google AdSense in the future which may use cookies to show relevant ads.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Vercel for hosting. Vercel may collect anonymized analytics like country, device type, and browser to improve performance.

4. Children's Privacy

Our service is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: hello@freepdfhub.example
